Under The Hood

Engineered for Data Sovereignty

How Peerloom Labs achieves real-time peer-to-peer sync without storing unencrypted user records on any server.

01

Local Cryptographic Identity

No sign-up emails or SMS OTPs. Your device creates an Ed25519 signing keypair stored in hardware-backed Android KeyStore. All operations are signed locally before broadcast.

Ed25519 • BIP-340 Schnorr
02

Blind Nostr Relay Transport

Inter-device packets sync through open public Nostr relays over secure WebSockets (WSS). All group payloads are end-to-end encrypted with 256-bit AES-GCM. Relays cannot read your expenses.

AES-256-GCM • WSS TLS 1.3
03

Integer Deterministic Ledger

Every financial value is stored strictly in integer minor units (cents/paise) with BigInteger proportional share distribution. Zero floating-point drift across heterogeneous devices.

Exact Minor Units • Zero Drift
