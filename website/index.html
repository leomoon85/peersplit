Studio Philosophy

The Peerloom Standard

Whether we are building peer-to-peer utilities or tactical strategy games, our core commitments remain uncompromised.

01

Absolute User Sovereignty

Your device is your territory. All saves, ledger operations, and data reside in your device's private sandbox. No forced sign-ins or cloud vendor lock-in.

02

Zero Surveillance & Ads

We reject ad SDKs, analytics beacons, telemetry hooks, and behavior tracking. Software should serve the user without monetization traps.

03

Native Craftsmanship

We avoid heavy webview wrappers when native code is needed. Our software starts instantly, conserves battery, and remains fast and reliable.
