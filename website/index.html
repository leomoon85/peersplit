Studio Foundations

The Peerloom Standard

Every utility and game we publish adheres strictly to three non-negotiable principles.

01

Absolute User Sovereignty

Your device is your domain. All records, ledger states, and game saves reside in your device's private sandbox. No forced email accounts or server dependencies.

02

Zero Surveillance & Ads

We reject third-party advertising SDKs, analytics beacons, telemetry hooks, and behavior tracking. Software should serve the user without monetization traps.

03

Native Craftsmanship

We avoid heavy webview shells when high performance is demanded. Our software boots instantaneously, respects battery life, and remains fast and reliable.
