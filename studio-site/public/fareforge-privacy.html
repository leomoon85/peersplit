Game Privacy Policy

Fareforge: Mobility Tycoon

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Local-Only Game Data

Fareforge stores all simulation progress, fleet statistics, company ledgers, and settings locally within your device's private application sandbox. No financial data, trip records, or save files are transmitted to or stored on any central server.

2. Zero Analytics, Ads & Trackers

Fareforge contains no third-party advertisements, tracking SDKs, user telemetry, analytics software, or in-app payment tracking. We do not track, monetize, or sell user data.

3. No Location Permissions

The game does not request or access your device's GPS or physical location. The world cities featured in the game (Bengaluru, Singapore, New York, London, Tokyo, San Francisco) are purely simulated game environments.

4. Street Maps & Network Usage

Fareforge can be played 100% offline using our original tactical vector board. If you optionally view street maps, the app fetches open map vector tiles from OpenFreeMap/MapLibre via standard HTTPS requests. No personal information or player accounts are sent to map providers.
